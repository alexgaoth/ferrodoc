# Numbered heading gets a tab

Section numbering inserts a `w:tab` between the number and the text, which
must read back as a space rather than gluing the words together.

## Second numbered heading

Term one
:   The definition of term one.

Term two
:   The definition of term two.

    A second paragraph of that definition.

Another term
:   Short.

Closing paragraph.
